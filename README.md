Sales Analytics

This repository contains Business Intelligence and Sales Analytics reports created using Excel/Power BI for AtliQ Hardware. The reports provide insights into sales, profitability, customer growth, and market performance.

Reports Included
1. P&L Statement by Months
Month-wise and quarterly financial analysis
Tracks Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin, and GM%
Compares FY 2019, FY 2020, and FY 2021 performance

2. P&L Statement by Fiscal Year
Yearly profit and loss summary
Financial comparison across fiscal years
Highlights revenue growth and margin trends

3. P&L Statement by Markets
Market-wise profitability analysis
Identifies top-performing and high-profitability regions
Supports business expansion decisions

4. Market Performance Vs Target
Compares actual sales against 2021 targets
Analyzes country-wise sales performance
Helps identify underperforming markets

5. Customer Performance Report
Customer-wise sales analysis from 2019–2021
Tracks revenue growth and top customers
Provides insights into customer contribution and business growth

Technologies Used

Sales Analytics
